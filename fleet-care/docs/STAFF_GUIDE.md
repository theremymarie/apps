# Staff guide

## Daily review

1. Open Overview and review due/overdue items and vehicles needing attention.
2. Use Vehicles to search by registration or name; filter Ready, Needs repair or Off road.
3. Select View / edit to review the vehicle and its maintenance history.
4. Open Renewal alerts for registration, insurance and service items within 30 days or overdue. Missing dates also require attention.

The dashboard counts due items, so one vehicle can contribute several alerts.

## Add or update a vehicle

Choose Add vehicle, enter its registration and name, select location and status, and enter the odometer and verified dates. Registration must be unique. Leave an unknown date blank rather than guessing it. Upload an optional vehicle photo, then Save vehicle.

To update an existing vehicle, choose View / edit. Due-date, name, location and status changes are recorded in its history. Use the maintenance note field to explain repairs and inspections. Select Add history entry to stage an entry; **Save vehicle is required to retain it**. Closing the dialog discards unsaved changes.

## Renewal workflow

After a renewal is completed, enter the new verified expiry date and add a note identifying the supporting document. An alert disappears from the 30-day list when the new date is more than 30 days ahead. An overdue date remains listed until updated. Registration, insurance and service are independent fields.

Preview reminder digest displays a simulated message only. It does not schedule or send email. Production reminders require a server-side scheduler and authenticated email provider.

## Backup

Export backup downloads all demo vehicle records, including photos and history, as JSON. Keep a backup before using Reset demo. Reset replaces all records with fictional sample data. Changes are specific to the browser and origin; colleagues will not see them on another device.

## Confirm before production import

Confirm the actual fleet count, whether the two additional photo registrations are active, the outstanding registration spelling mismatch, and any missing vehicle. Verify each vehicle's inspection issues, expiry dates, odometer, photo and responsible staff member from source documents. None of the unresolved fleet details were imported into this public mockup.
